2.31.3.15         CT_WindowViewport

Target namespace: http://schemas.microsoft.com/office/drawing/2017/model3d

Referenced by: CT_Model3D

Represents that the model is in window mode. In this mode, the size of the frame is manually
modifiable and is preserved. The camera can also be panned, causing the model to potentially be

the model in the center of the frame.
